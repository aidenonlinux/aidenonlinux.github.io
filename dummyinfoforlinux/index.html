Package managers

Based on your distro, your package manager can differ, such as apt for Debian (also Ubuntu, Mint and more) and pacman for Arch-based distros (Arch, Archcraft, Cachy).

Root commands

How to install Arch

Quick overview:

  1. Download the Arch ISO and flash it to a USB.
  2. Boot from the USB and open the live environment.
  3. Run archinstall if you want the easiest install.
  4. Choose your username, password, and desktop environment.
  5. Select a bootloader like GRUB and install it to your disk.
  6. Reboot and log in to your new Arch system.

If you have determination like Frisk in Undertale, you can do it manually. If not, archinstall is the easier route. Pick a DE you actually want: GNOME for simplicity, KDE Plasma for a Windows-like feel, Hyprland if you want something more custom, or no DE if you want a minimal setup.

Desktop environments

Popular DEs to choose from:

  • GNOME — clean, modern, simple, and beginner-friendly.
  • KDE Plasma — very customizable and feels close to Windows.
  • Xfce — lightweight, fast, and resource-friendly.
  • Cinnamon — classic layout with a familiar desktop feel.
  • Mate — lightweight and classic, great for older hardware.
  • Hyprland — tiled and highly customizable, best for power users.
  • Budgie — sleek and elegant with a simple workflow.
  • LXQt — very lightweight and efficient.

Pick one based on how you want your system to feel: simple, custom, lightweight, or Windows-like.
